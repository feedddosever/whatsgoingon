Verdict key:
- **CONFIRMED**: the governing or official document says what the row says.
- **CORRECTED**: the document contradicts or materially narrows the row. A replacement sentence is given.
- **PARTIAL**: the core mechanism is confirmed, but specific figures or conditions in the row could not be found in any document I opened.
- **UNVERIFIABLE**: the governing document could not be reached, or the row asserts a negative.

Confidence achievable:
- **statute**: I read the statute or regulation text itself.
- **published**: I read an official board, agency or system policy page.
- **needs_check**: neither of the above.

Where the "fix" is to drop an unverified clause, the corrected sentence below already drops it.
