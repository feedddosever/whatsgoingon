1. **Cash-first Web3 flagships are the core of the plan.** Colosseum ($840,000 in prizes + $2.5M seed funding), Monad Metropolis ($250,000+), and Arbitrum Singapore ($115,000) are all online, solo-friendly, EVM-centric, and pay in cash/stablecoin. This removes what would normally be Kei's highest-EV, fastest-payout channel. Ongoing bug bounties (Immunefi/Cantina) remain but are not time-boxed competitions.
3. **AI/ML cash is concentrated in a few large pools.** Kaggle ARC Prize ($2M total across tracks) and Kaggriculture ($50k) pay real money via wire/Stripe but are extremely competitive and open-source-mandatory; Nebius×NVIDIA ($50k) and RevenueCat Shipaton ($740k cash) are the biggest genuinely-cash AI pools.
